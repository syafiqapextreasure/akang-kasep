OUR STORY

A Terengganu company with a clear purpose.

Akang Kasep Sdn. Bhd. undertakes construction and maintenance work. Our aim is to deliver innovative and creative solutions through technology, technical knowledge and effective management.

We bring together local professionals from different disciplines, combining practical expertise with project management experience to support the needs of our clients.

  1. 11 Jan 2012 Incorporated as Agenda Tarbus Sdn. Bhd.
  2. 20 May 2020 Renamed Akang Kasep Sdn. Bhd.
