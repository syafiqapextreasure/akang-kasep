01

Information you provide

When you send a project enquiry, we receive your name, company (if supplied), email address or phone number, service required, project location and project brief. Please do not include identity-document numbers, bank information or sensitive personal information in your message.

02

How information is used

Your details are used to review your enquiry, contact you about the proposed work and keep a record of the conversation. Submitting an enquiry does not subscribe you to a marketing list.

03

Service providers

This website is hosted on Cloudflare. Enquiry records are stored in the site’s Cloudflare D1 database when the enquiry service is configured. Cloudflare may process technical request information to operate and protect the service. No advertising pixels or third-party analytics are included in this website. The Contact page embeds Google Maps to show our office building. When the map loads, Google receives technical information such as your IP address and browser details and may use cookies under its Privacy Policy.

04

Keeping information

Website enquiry records are retained for up to 90 days under the configured housekeeping policy. Records required for an ongoing business relationship may be retained separately by the company. A one-way, daily request identifier is used briefly to limit spam; raw IP addresses are not stored in the enquiry database.

05

Your enquiries

Contact us to ask about information provided through this website, request a correction or discuss deletion. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. If the website form is unavailable, you can contact us directly by phone or email.
